About



Growing up in Trinidad and Tobago, my interest in politics has been shaped by my country's oil-and-gas based economy, its persistent problem with crime, and Venezuela (our closest neighbour).



My studies in Mathematics and Government at Dartmouth College, along with my experience working in political research, corporate strategy, and NGO management, have furthered my desire to understand politics, violence, and Caribbean security. I employ both quantitative and qualitative methods to investigate these topics. Read my analyses here.



To contact me, or for a more thorough look at my career, visit my LinkedIn here.



Outside of analysis, my biggest passion is photography. For as long as I can remember, I've been enamoured by photography's ability to capture the beauty of fleeting moments. A collection of my work can be found here.
